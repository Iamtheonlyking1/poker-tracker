1. The short version

  • No account = nothing leaves your device. Without an account, all your games and notes are stored only in your browser's local storage. We never receive them.
  • An account stores your email and syncs your data to our servers so you can use it on more than one device.
  • We use a small number of processors: a hosting/database provider, a payment processor, an AI provider (only when you use an AI feature), and a website host/CDN.
  • We do not sell your data, do not run advertising, and do not use your content to train AI models.
  • You can export or delete your data at any time.

3. What we collect

3.1 If you use Poker Night without an account

Your games, results, player names and notes, custom ranges, tournament structures, and settings are stored in your browser using localStorage on your device. This data is not transmitted to us. The website is served as static files; our host may log standard technical request data (see 3.4).

3.2 Account data

DataWhy
Email address To create your account, sign you in (one-time codes / links), send service and billing notices, and respond to support.
Display name and (optional) avatar To show who you are in shared games and on your account screen.
Sign-in method identifiers (if you use Google or Apple sign-in) To link your login to your account. We receive a stable identifier and your email/name from the provider; we do not receive your password.
A device identifier we generate To sync reliably across your devices and resolve conflicts. It is not linked to any hardware ID and stays on the device.

3.3 Content you sync

When you have an account, the data described in 3.1 is copied to our servers so it is available on your other devices and can be restored. This includes anything you choose to type in: game names, buy-in and cash-out amounts, chip counts, settlement records, tournament data, and free-text notes about players. Only put information here that you are comfortable storing and that you are entitled to record about other people.

3.4 Technical and usage data

  • Log data from our website host and backend: IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser and device type, pages/endpoints accessed, timestamps, and referrer. Used for security, abuse prevention, debugging, and understanding load.
  • Error reports: if the app hits an error, it may send us a diagnostic record (error message, a stack trace, the app version, and coarse context such as which screen you were on). We aim to keep personal data out of these; occasionally a note or name could appear in a stack trace.
  • Feature usage counters needed to enforce plan limits and meter AI usage (for example, how many games are synced, how many AI requests you have made this month).

3.5 Payment data

If you subscribe to Pro, payment is processed by Razorpay. Razorpay collects and processes your card, UPI, or bank details directly — we do not see or store your full payment details. We receive from Razorpay a payment/subscription identifier, the status, the amount, the last 4 digits or payment-method type, and the billing contact you provide, which we keep for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes.

3.6 AI feature inputs

If you use an AI feature (for example, "ask the coach about this hand"), the specific information you submit for that request — such as the hand history you paste or select — is sent to Anthropic to generate a response. See section 6.

  • AI features are optional and only run when you trigger them.

11. How we protect data

  • Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Data at rest in our database and backups is encrypted by our hosting provider.
  • Access to production data is limited to people who need it, protected by authentication and, where available, row-level security so one account cannot read another's data.
  • Payment details are handled by a PCI-DSS-compliant processor, not by us.
  • We keep dependencies updated and follow reasonable secure-development practices.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause you harm, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.
